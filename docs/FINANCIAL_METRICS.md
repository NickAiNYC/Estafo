# ESTAFO — Financial Metrics & Commercial Accounting Rules

This document establishes the accounting principles and ledger rules enforced within the ESTAFO console to ensure strict financial integrity and prevent misleading representations of value.

---

## 1. The Four Buckets of Value

To prevent conflating prospective litigation windfalls with actual operating income, all monetary quantities in ESTAFO are strictly segregated into four discrete tiers:

```
[ Tier 1: Realized Cash ] <--- Bank Settlement Confirmed
         ^
[ Tier 2: Invoiced AR ]   <--- Formal Invoice Delivered to Law Firm / Agency
         ^
[ Tier 3: Committed Work ]<--- Executed Research Pilot Agreement
         ^
[ Tier 4: Pipeline / Opp ]<--- Unadjudicated Theoretical Value (Bounties / Qui Tam)
```

### Definitions:
1. **Cash Collected (Realized Revenue)**:
   - Money actually deposited into bank accounts from paid research pilots, completed consulting engagements, or disbursed statutory citizen bounties.
   - **Accounting Rule**: Only cash collected counts toward Gross Revenue and Net Profit.
2. **Invoiced Accounts Receivable (Committed AR)**:
   - Invoices issued to law firms following delivery of an evidence packet or research dossier.
3. **Signed Contract Value**:
   - Total contract value of signed pilot or research agreements awaiting milestone completion.
4. **Pipeline Exposure (Speculative)**:
   - Theoretical recovery amounts or statutory penalty maximums.
   - **Prohibited Action**: Never displayed in the top-line Revenue metric. Must always be tagged as `SPECULATIVE STATUTORY EXPOSURE`.

## 2. Unit Economics & Ledger Metrics

For each commercial stream, ESTAFO tracks:
- **Direct Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Data API subscriptions, serverless execution costs, research staff time.
- **Gross Margin**:
  $$\text{Gross Margin} = \frac{\text{Cash Collected} - \text{Direct Investigation Costs}}{\text{Cash Collected}}$$
- **Cycle Time**: Days elapsed from initial Socrata anomaly detection to final cash receipt.
- **Conversion Rate by Signal**: Ratio of leads generated by a dataset (e.g. 311 Idling vs DOB Violations) to paying customer engagements.

## 3. Commercial Law Firm Pilot Offer

ESTAFO monetizes actionable intelligence through structured law firm research engagements:
- **Pilot Package**: "Medicaid Provider Peer Deviation Dossier" or "NYC Construction Risk Dossier".
- **Standard Retainer**: $7,500 fixed fee per research engagement.
- **Deliverable**: Source-verified evidence packet, chronological timeline, geocoded site records, and counter-evidence analysis.
- **Customer Success Metric**: Analyst hours saved per litigation lead.
